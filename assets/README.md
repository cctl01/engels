# Cartoon-Style Harry Potter Illustrations

Add your cartoon-style renders here. Each image should match the filename used in the JSON data files.

## Style Guide

- **Format**: PNG with transparent background
- **Size**: 128x128px (or 256x256px for higher resolution)
- **Style**: Colorful cartoon illustrations with a magical Harry Potter feel
- **Examples**:
  - `dog.png` — A cartoon dog wearing a wizard hat
  - `cat.png` — A cartoon cat with a pointed hat
  - `bird.png` — A snowy owl (like Hedwig!)
  - `fish.png` — A magical glowing fish
  - `horse.png` — A hippogriff or magical horse
  - `red.png` — A red Gryffindor scarf or crest
  - `blue.png` — A blue Ravenclaw scarf or crest
  - `green.png` — A green Slytherin scarf or crest
  - `yellow.png` — A yellow Hufflepuff scarf or crest

## Tips

- Use warm golds, deep purples, and rich colors to match the magical theme
- Keep illustrations simple and recognizable for an 8-year-old
- Consider adding subtle magical elements (sparkles, stars, glowing effects)
